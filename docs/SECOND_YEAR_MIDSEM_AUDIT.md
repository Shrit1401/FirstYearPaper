# Second-year midsem audit

Reviewed 22 September 2026. All 37 supplied PDFs (275 pages) had native text extraction and Tesseract OCR (English, PSM 3, approximately 202 dpi). All source first pages were visually checked, with question/solution content checks. Handwritten mathematics and diagrams remain in the original scans; OCR is not presented as a verified transcription.

## Result

- 17 question-paper PDFs, including two sections combined into one Engineering Mathematics III 2023 paper.
- 8 PDFs containing both questions and answers.
- 12 solution PDFs: 11 paired with their corresponding question papers and one unpaired ECE maths scheme.
- 25 exam sets: 21 in CSE (including related IT/CCE/DSE courses), 4 in ECE.
- No supplied PDF was identified as an endsem. Some are sessional/internal exams. Course/date metadata based only on filenames is noted in the catalog.

The earlier second-year archive and branch navigation have been restored alongside this midsem collection. Mathematics is accessible from each Semester 3 branch, including EnC, through the shared subject archive. Midsem imports now merge into the archive rather than replacing it. Repeat remains hidden; its separate practice catalog still excludes endsem-derived sets. Other academic years and original archive files are preserved. Source PDFs retain their original exam formats; the generated practice-paper MCQ rule is not imposed on past papers.

## Qualifications

- MAT 2152 (ECE) has no complete question paper or printed date. The course code is supplied by the filename; the subject is inferred from the mathematical content.
- CSE 2122 (2023) has course/date information in the filename, not a complete exam header.
- MAT 2126 (2023) has separate MCQ and theory PDFs. They are combined in that order, with both originals retained in the source metadata.
- MAT 2155 (2022) is a second sessional, labelled accordingly.
- MAT 2126 (2024) is identified as midterm by its matching evaluation scheme.
- MAT 2101 (2025) date is supported by the matching scheme and filename.
- CSS 2104 (2025) has a matching scheme that prints CSE 2104. The question paper course code is used, and the discrepancy is shown.
- ICT and DSE papers retain their course codes and program labels. They are not paired with CSE papers merely because the subjects match.

## Reimport

The reviewed manifest in `scripts/second-year-midsem-review.json` records SHA-256 hashes, original paths, page counts and classifications. `scripts/import-second-year-midsems.py --ece <folder> --cse <folder>` verifies all source hashes, copies the originals, combines the two maths sections and updates the catalog. Run `scripts/refresh-midsem-assets.py` and `scripts/upload-convex-assets.mjs` after importing to publish new assets. The main manifest generator merges this collection without replacing the older archive.
