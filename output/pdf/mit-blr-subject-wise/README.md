# MIT Bengaluru: subject-wise PYQs

Maximum four latest distinct dated exam papers per course code. Course codes checked in PDF text; exact printed dates used where present. Some midsem dates come from the supplied archive filenames and are explicitly marked in index.csv. Question-paper and combined-question/answer files are eligible; standalone solutions and generated practice papers are excluded.

Semester 3 targets the five subjects in public/midsem/index.json. Older courses with different codes are not silently treated as equivalent. Semester 1 retains separate course-code folders for the older and newer curricula because no first-year branch/scheme was specified. Select the codes on your timetable.

Source: existing MIT Bengaluru archive plus recovered downloads from https://paper.shrit.in. The new official library download endpoint requires SSO. Campus provenance follows the MIT Bengaluru archive; a generic MAHE header alone is not independent campus proof.

This is the latest available in the checked collection, not a claim of exhaustive global coverage. Original full papers are retained, including endsem topics beyond the midsem syllabus.
